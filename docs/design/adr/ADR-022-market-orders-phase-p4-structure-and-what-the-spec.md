# ADR-022 Market orders (phase P4): structure, and what the spec left open

- **Context:** P4 adds the planning lines, the live preview and Buy/Sell on top of the P1 Domain, the P2 gateway and the P3 panel. Implementing it left points open, and three places where documents disagreed: 09 put Add Pend Line in P5 while P4's Definition of Done includes T-03 (Add Line → Add Pend Line → Add Line → Delete Line); 05 §5 places lines with `InpDefaultOffsetPct`, which 07 did not list; T-25 asks for exactly one position after a fast double click, but with synchronous requests (ADR-010) a click made while the first order is being sent is processed only after it returns, later than the 800 ms cooldown on a slow server (Dukascopy MT4 sometimes answers after 7–11 s). Running the trade steps on the demo accounts also showed a P3 defect: every dimmed (F-33) button was drawn almost white on MT4.
- **Decision:**
  - Files outside the CLAUDE.md §4 layout (split like ADR-019/021 to stay near ~600 lines): `App/CommandsTrade.mqh` (OP-03/04, part of `CCommands`), `App/ControllerPreview.mqh` (preview, labels, line sync, drops, screenshots; part of `CController`), `App/UiSelfTestTrade.mqh` + `UiSelfTestTradeLines.mqh` + `UiSelfTestTradeOrders.mqh` (debug only).
  - **P4 does Add Pend Line (OP-05) and Pending Info (OP-06) as display only**, so T-03 passes as written and Lines has one mode state machine from the start. Set Pending, Del Pending, the overlay and OP-16 stay in P5. 09 updated. The camera button (OP-14, promised by the P3 panel's "coming in phase P4") works too; screenshot naming is final per 06 OP-14 (local time in the name).
  - `InpDefaultOffsetPct` (15 %) added to the inputs and to 07, range 1–45 % (at 1.5 × the pending SL distance stays inside the view).
  - Default placement (05 §5 refined, pure Domain functions `CPlanner::MarketLines / PendingLines / FollowTp`, unit-tested): the distance is at least the stops level + 2 ticks; the 5 % clamp is not applied when it would put a line on the wrong side of the price or inside the stops level, nor when the price itself is out of view; prices rounded away from the price. A dropped line snaps to the tick grid.
  - The TP line's "placed by the user" flag (`InpTpFollowsSl`) lives in the line's tooltip, so it survives a restart with the line.
  - Drawing order: chart objects are drawn in creation order, so a line created after the panel crossed it. Whenever RiskDesk creates a line or label, `CPanel::Raise()` creates the panel objects again (one redraw, no flicker).
  - The lines on the chart decide the mode on `OnInit` (adoption after a timeframe change or restart); a line deleted by hand drops to a consistent mode with an amber note (05 §5).
  - Preview (OP-15): recomputed only when the lines, the quote, Risk %, the balance (equity when it is the risk base) or the spec changed. The SL alone decides the lots; a manual TP line on the wrong side makes the trade invalid (`—`) but keeps the SL label's risk. F-33 dims each side whose full plan (side, TP, BC-07) is invalid; the tooltip says why.
  - Buy/Sell order of checks (06 OP-03): permissions, spec, quote, Risk %, lines, plan, BC-05 valuation, netting state, MT5 volume limit, lots, caps, margin, confirmations, G-01. The status after a fill shows the risk at the **fill price and the SL on the server** (it can differ from the preview by the slippage).
  - **Netting (BC-10):** a same-direction add is checked as one position of (old + new) lots at the averaged open price with the new SL, replacing the old contribution, and always asks for confirmation. An opposite order is refused unless `InpNettingAllowOpposite`; then, after its own confirmation, it is done as G-04 (close min(lots, position)) followed, for a reversal, by G-01 (the rest with the lines' SL/TP). A single opposite deal was not used: the MT5 ambiguity check of an opening deal (BC-14) looks for `DEAL_ENTRY_IN/INOUT`, so a reducing deal (`DEAL_ENTRY_OUT`) after a timeout would not be recognised and could be sent twice (rule R9). The gateway keeps refusing a direct opposite open.
  - Margin (F-31): free margin minus `InpMarginBufferPct` against `MarginFor`; refusal "Not enough margin: needs X margin for L lot, Y free (b % kept unused) - max about M lot at the current leverage"; `InpReduceToMargin` sends M (amber). A margin the terminal cannot compute is left to the server (WARN in the log).
  - Confirmations: `MessageBox` Yes/No; the Strategy Tester has no dialogs, so it answers Yes there with a log line; debug builds let the self-test answer.
  - **Click cooldown (T-25):** also counted from the end of the command, so a click queued during a slow request is ignored.
  - **Colour blend on MQL4:** `(c & 0xFF) - (d & 0xFF)` is unsigned in MQL4 (the hex literal), so the subtraction wrapped around and `CTheme::Blend` returned colours like `0x66E6E7DA`; channels are now extracted into signed ints first. The self-test checks the dimmed colour against an independent value.
  - **Trade self-test** (08 §3e): `run-uitest.ps1 -Suite trade | trade-basic [-Permissions off]`; real orders on demo accounts only (R13), magic 772288, own sandbox, every test position closed again. T-27 runs when the chart symbol is outside its trading session (`CMarket::InSession`, the terminal's session table; information only, RiskDesk still does not block, BC-17).
- **Consequences:** T-03…T-12 and T-25…T-28 are checked unattended on every demo account and symbol (08 §3e); the owner keeps T-24 and real mouse/keyboard feel. P5 adds order placement to existing pending lines. The opt-in netting reversal is two requests, so between them the account is flat for a moment; the confirmation says so ("closes … and opens …").
