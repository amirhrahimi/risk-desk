# ADR-026 Trade management (phase P6): Risk Free, Half Close, Chart Close, Full Close

- **Context:** P6 adds OP-09 Risk Free, OP-10 Half Close, OP-11 Chart Close and OP-12 Full Close on top of the P2 gateway (G-03 and G-04 existed with the BC-07 freeze checks and the BC-10 netting close, and had passed GatewaySmoke on A1–A6) and the P1 Domain (`CPlanner::RiskFree`, `CRiskMath::PartialClose`, V-18 to V-20 and V-23). The spec left open how the status reads when several positions end differently, which colour such a click gets, how a command that sends many requests shows its progress while the requests are synchronous (ADR-010), how the commission distance and the booked costs of 04 §9 are valued, what happens to a position larger than the maximum volume per request, and how the four buttons can be tested on real accounts without touching anyone else's trades.
- **Decision:**
  - Files outside the CLAUDE.md §4 layout (split like ADR-019/021/022/025): `App/CommandsManage.mqh` (OP-09..OP-12, part of `CCommands`), `App/UiSelfTestManage.mqh` + `UiSelfTestManageClose.mqh` (debug only). `Platform/History.mqh` gains `BookedCost` (open position's commission + swap, for `RF_COMMISSION`).
  - **Progress:** `CProgressSink` in `App/Commands.mqh` has one virtual method, `Progress(text)`, implemented by `CController` (status bar + panel redraw, no log line). `CCommands` calls it before each request when a command has more than one item: `Closing 4/9...`, `Deleting 1/2 pending orders...`, `Half Close: closing 1/2...`, `Risk Free: 2/3...`. It is RiskDesk's only virtual method: ADR-001 and 02 §4 avoid virtual dispatch for choosing the platform, which this is not; without it Commands would have to include the Controller, which already includes Commands.
  - **Per-item results (D33):** tickets are collected first and each is selected again before acting; a position gone meanwhile is "already closed" (amber), never a failure. The status starts with the counts and lists every item that was skipped or failed with its reason; Risk Free and Half Close also list what they did (`#123 SL 1.09800 -> 1.10012`, `#126 0.01 of 0.03 lot, rest 0.02 is now #130`). The status bar shows the first three lines; the tooltip and the log line have all of it. Colours: green only when everything in scope was done, amber when anything was skipped, gone or carries a note (and when Risk Free moved nothing), red when nothing was done because of failures. Nothing in scope: amber with the button's name.
  - **Domain:** `CPlanner::RiskFree` reports its skip reason as `SRiskFreeResult.skip` (`ERfSkip`), so the command counts by reason without parsing text; `CRiskMath::CloseChunk` splits a full close above `SYMBOL_VOLUME_MAX` (D35). Both unit-tested.
  - **Risk Free (D34):** one fresh quote and a fresh select per position; the commission distance from the BC-05 value of a 1000-tick move (only when a commission is set); `RF_COMMISSION` from `CHistory::BookedCost`. G-03 keeps the TP. A G-03 that fails for a position that has gone meanwhile counts as "already closed". No confirmation (F-28 lists none for Risk Free).
  - **Half Close:** plans every position first (04 §10), then asks `InpConfirmHalfClose` with what is closed and what stays (D36), then G-04 each. MT4's new ticket for the rest is named in the status; it is not in the collected list, so one click never halves a position twice (BC-16).
  - **Chart Close / Full Close:** G-04 for the whole position, in parts above the maximum volume (D35). Full Close groups by symbol, confirms by default with the positions per symbol, the floating P/L and the pending orders it will delete, and deletes the pending orders (`InpFullCloseAlsoPending`) after the positions. The P/L in the status is the gateway's for each close (`STradeOutcome.profit`: the closing deal or the closed MT4 order, profit + swap + commission); when the gateway could not read it for some closes the status says `(2 of 3 closes)` instead of guessing.
  - **Freeze levels (BC-07):** checked where the request is built (G-03, G-04, G-05) and, for Risk Free, already in the plan ("frozen", amber). Every demo account reports a freeze level of 0, so only the Domain tests exercise it.
  - **Debug manage suite** (`run-uitest.ps1 -Suite manage`, 08 §3g): real minimum-lot test positions opened through the gateway, magic 772288 ("RiskDesk") and 772289 ("manual"), demo accounts only (R13). When anyone else's positions are on the chart symbol the chart buttons are put in tool scope and the manual part is skipped; when anyone else's trades are on the account Full Close is put in tool scope; so the suite never touches another trade. T-16 cannot choose the market price: it opens two buys and two sells, waits (≤ 180 s) until both positions of one side would be moved with a margin, sets one of them to break-even by hand and clicks Risk Free (1 moved, 1 already protected, the other side skipped). The panel suite clicks the four buttons only when nothing is in their scope, Full Close otherwise with its confirmation answered No, so it still never trades.
- **Consequences:** Every panel button works (`CCommands::Implemented` is true for all). T-16..T-19, T-30 (Half Close and Risk Free part) and T-32 (one chart) run unattended on every demo account. A click on a management button can take several seconds with many positions; the panel shows each step and stays busy (further clicks are ignored, 06 §0). 06 OP-09..OP-12, 08 §3d/§3g and 11 D33–D36 updated.
