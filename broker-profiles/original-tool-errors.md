# Original tool errors

Experts and Journal lines from the original tool at the brokers where it fails (docs/03-broker-compatibility.md §1). Every entry becomes a regression case for T-36.

How to record one:
1. Open the Toolbox (Ctrl+T) on the terminal where the original fails.
2. Click Buy (or the failing button) with the original tool.
3. In the **Experts** tab and then the **Journal** tab: right-click > Copy, or select the last lines and press Ctrl+C.
4. Paste them below under a new heading. "No line at all" is also a result: write it down.

Do not paste passwords or real-account numbers. Demo logins are fine.
